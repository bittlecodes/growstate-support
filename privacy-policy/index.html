Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

GrowState is designed to keep your plant records private and under your control. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information you add to GrowState

GrowState stores information that you choose to enter, including plant names, grow spaces, stage dates, care records, notes, harvest and inventory records, consumption entries, and app preferences. The app may also store plant photos that you capture or select.

Where your information is stored

Your GrowState information is stored on your device. When iCloud is available for the app, Apple CloudKit may synchronize that information through the private iCloud account signed in on your devices. GrowState does not require a separate developer-operated account, and the developer does not operate a separate server that receives your plant records.

Apple's handling of iCloud information is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

Camera and photo access

GrowState requests camera access only when you choose to photograph a plant. You may also choose an existing image using Apple's system photo picker. Selected or captured images are stored with the related plant record. You can deny or revoke camera access in your device settings.

Backups and exports

GrowState can create JSON backups and CSV exports when you request them. You choose where those files are saved or shared. Once an exported file leaves GrowState, its storage, sharing, and deletion are controlled by you and by the destination you selected.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

GrowState does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the developer does not sell your personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Your records remain in GrowState until you edit or delete them. Removing information from the app may also synchronize that deletion through iCloud. Copies contained in backups or exports must be deleted separately from the locations where you saved them. Apple may retain synchronized information according to its own iCloud policies.

Children's privacy

GrowState is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when GrowState's features or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to GrowState@icloud.com.
