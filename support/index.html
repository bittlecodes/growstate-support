Help with GrowState

Support

Need help with GrowState? Send a message and include enough detail to reproduce the problem.

Email GrowState@icloud.com

When reporting a problem

Please include:

  • Your iPhone, iPad, or Mac model and operating-system version.
  • The GrowState version and build shown in GrowState Settings.
  • The steps that led to the problem and what you expected to happen.
  • A screenshot, when useful, with any private information removed.

iCloud synchronization

Confirm that each device is signed in to the intended Apple Account and that iCloud is enabled. Keep GrowState open briefly on a reliable network after making changes. Synchronization time can vary depending on device and network conditions.

Back up your records

Open the More Actions menu in GrowState and choose Export Full Backup. Save the JSON backup somewhere you control before deleting the app, changing devices, or testing a major update. Use Import Full Backup to restore a compatible GrowState backup.

Camera access

If the camera is unavailable, open the system Settings app, find GrowState, and confirm that camera access is enabled. You can also choose an existing photo from the system photo picker.

Feedback

Feature suggestions and usability feedback are welcome at GrowState@icloud.com.
